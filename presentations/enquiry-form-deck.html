01
說明對象In scope

官網 Enquiry。名、姓、公司、電子郵件、電話有星號。國家、服務、訊息、條款沒有星號。 The public Enquiry. First name, last name, company, email, and phone carry an asterisk. Country, services, message, and terms do not.

02

系統句：System lines: 英文頁與 /zh-tw/contact 都顯示同一組英文成功句與失敗句。簡報引用原文，不另寫一套中文系統訊息。 The English page and /zh-tw/contact show the same English success and error lines. This deck quotes them and does not invent a Chinese system message.

08 / 中英文Languages

步驟相同，敘事切換 Same steps, two narrations

表單順序不因語系改變。變的是這份簡報的說法，以及欄位的中文名稱。 The form order does not change with language. What changes is this deck’s narration and the Chinese field names.

01
步驟Steps

開啟、必填、國家與服務、條款、送出、結果。順序固定。 Open, required fields, country and services, terms, submit, result. The order stays fixed.

02
名稱Labels

工具列語言按鈕切換敘事。服務中文名使用本站的貨運物流、專案物流、倉儲物流、船務代理。 The toolbar language button switches the narration. Chinese service names use this site’s 貨運物流、專案物流、倉儲物流、船務代理.

03
系統句System lines

成功句與 Oops 維持官網英文，不隨簡報語系改寫。 The thank-you line and Oops stay in the published English, including while this deck is in Chinese.

怎麼切：How to switch: 現在按鈕顯示「中文」。按下去改為英文，按鈕改顯示 EN。再按一次回到中文。 The button shows the language you are reading. Choose it to switch. EN means this deck is in English.
